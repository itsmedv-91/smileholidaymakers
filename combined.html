Day-by-Day Journey Timeline

Day 1 Welcome to Shimla & Historic Mall Road walk ▼

Arrive at Chandigarh airport or railway station. Meet our dedicated professional driver and embark on a scenic drive up the winding green hills to Shimla. After checking in to your room and resting, enjoy a relaxed evening walk along the historic Mall Road, the Ridge, Scandal Point, and Christ Church. Enjoy dinner at your hotel.

Private Sedan Transfer Welcome Drink Buffet Dinner
Day 2 Kufri Valley Excursion & Jakhoo Temple heights ▼

After breakfast, take a scenic drive to Kufri, nestled at 8,250 feet. Explore the Green Valley, Indira Tourist Park, and enjoy optional horse rides or adventure park swings. Later, head back to Shimla and climb Jakhoo Hill, the highest peak in Shimla, to pay respects at the giant 108ft Hanuman Statue and witness panoramic sunset views over the Shivalik ranges.

Kufri Sightseeing Jakhoo Cab Support Breakfast & Dinner
Day 3 Scenic transfer to Manali via Kullu Valley & River Rafting ▼

Check out after breakfast and proceed on the scenic drive to Manali (approx. 7-8 hours). Travel along the roaring Beas river. En route, stop in Kullu Valley. Visit shawl weaving factories, and take part in an exciting river rafting ride. Reach Manali by evening, check-in to your hotel, and relax by the balcony.

Intercity Transfer Kullu Stopover Rafting Access Breakfast & Dinner
Day 4 Manali Local Heritage & Vashisht Hot Springs ▼

Spend a relaxed day exploring the local heritage of Manali. Visit the 16th-century Hadimba Temple, set inside deep pine-forested sanctuary grounds. Explore the ancient Manu Temple, and take a dip in the natural therapeutic Vashisht Hot Springs. Spend your evening strolling and shopping for local wooden souvenirs on Mall Road.

Local Guided Tour Vashisht Access Breakfast & Dinner
Day 5 Solang Valley Excursion & Rohtang Pass Snow Point ▼

Wake up early for an adventure-filled day. Drive up to Solang Valley, famous for its majestic snow slopes and adventure activities. Try paragliding, zorbing, and take the ropeway ride. If permits permit, proceed up to the mighty Rohtang Pass (13,058ft) to walk on thick glacier ice and see the breathtaking peaks of Lahaul valley.

Solang Valley Tour Rohtang Pass Permit assistance Breakfast & Dinner
Day 6 Kasol Day Trip & Manikaran Hot Springs ▼

Embark on an excursion to the beautiful Parvati Valley. Visit the quaint village of Kasol, popular for its riverside cafes and scenic pine forests. Later, visit Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara, known for its natural geothermal hot springs, and enjoy a traditional community meal (Langar).

Parvati Valley Excursion Manikaran Entry Breakfast & Dinner
Day 7 Departure drop-off to Chandigarh ▼

After breakfast, check out of your hotel. Drive back down the winding valley roads from Manali to Chandigarh (approx 8-9 hours). Our driver will drop you at the airport or station for your journey home, leaving you with big smiles and lifelong travel memories.

Departing Transfer Airport/Station Drop Breakfast
