Day-by-Day Journey Timeline

Day 1 Delhi to Shimla & Hotel Check-in ▼

Board the Luxury AC Volvo from Delhi for a scenic overnight journey to Shimla. Upon arrival in Shimla, enjoy a complimentary hotel transfer, check-in, freshen up, and spend the day relaxing. Dinner is included at the hotel.

Luxury AC Volvo Overnight Travel Dinner Included
Day 2 Local Shimla Sightseeing & Mall Road Stroll ▼

Enjoy breakfast at the hotel. Embark on a private cab tour of Shimla's best sights: The Ridge, Mall Road, Lakkar Bazaar, Christ Church, Sankat Mochan Hanuman Ji, and Jakhu Temple. Enjoy a free evening to stroll Mall Road at your own pace.

Private Cab Tour Shimla Sightseeing Breakfast & Dinner
Day 3 Kufri Hills Excursion & Transfer to Manali ▼

After breakfast, check out and explore Kufri's beautiful mountain scenery, pine forests, and nature trails. Later, enjoy a private cab transfer to Manali (7-8 hours) driving along the beautiful Kullu Valley. Check-in at your Manali hotel with non-alcoholic welcome drinks.

Kufri Hills Excursion Private Cab Transfer Breakfast & Dinner
Day 4 Local Manali Sightseeing & Beas River Vistas ▼

Breakfast at the hotel, followed by local sightseeing. Visit Hidimba Devi Temple, Club House, Vashisht Temple (with therapeutic hot springs), Jugni Waterfall Trek, Tibetan Monastery, and Van Vihar. Leisure evening spent exploring Manali Mall Road.

Hadimba & Vashisht Jugni Waterfall Trek Breakfast & Dinner
Day 5 Solang Valley Adventure & Scenic Viewpoints ▼

Breakfast at the hotel. Drive 15 km to Solang Valley, the adventure capital. Enjoy activities like paragliding, cable car rides, zorbing, snow scooter rides, skiing, horse riding, or the optional Anjani Mahadev Trek.

Solang Valley Excursion Adventure Sports Point Breakfast & Dinner
Day 6 Kullu Valley Tour, Manikaran Sahib & Return ▼

After breakfast, check out of the hotel. Tour Kullu (Naggar Castle, Roerich Art Gallery, Kais Gompa, Shawl Factory, and Rafting point) and visit the hot springs of Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara. In the evening, board the Luxury AC Volvo from Manali back to Delhi.

Kullu & Manikaran Tour Volvo Departure to Delhi Breakfast Included
