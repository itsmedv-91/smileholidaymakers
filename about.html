About Our Agency

The faces and philosophy behind Smile Holiday Makers. We manage the details so you can focus on the mountains.

The "Smile" Philosophy

At SMILE HOLIDAY MAKERS, we believe travel should be a source of pure happiness, not logistical anxiety. Planning a mountain trip to high-altitude destinations like Manali and Shimla involves complex details: obtaining Rohtang snow passes, booking safe local drivers who know landslide-prone zones, selecting hotels with proper heating, and arranging transfers across cities.

We founded our agency with a simple goal: to manage all logistics seamlessly so that every client has nothing but a wide, relaxed smile on their face. From the moment we pick you up at Chandigarh to the final departure drop, our local ground teams in Shimla and Manali monitor your trip details, hotel comfort, and driver schedules in real-time.
